# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## My Pledge

I am committed to making these projects a welcoming, safe, and inclusive place for all contributors and participants.

## My Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Provide helpful, kind feedback.
- There’s no place here for insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you see behaviour that goes against these values, please contact me directly via GitHub Issues or profile contact.

Thank you for helping me to keep this a safe and supportive space!
– Sam Newhouse
